Features
⏱
Live Urgency Countdown
Every assignment shows a live countdown that shifts from green to red as the deadline approaches. You always know exactly how much time you have.
📚
Subjects with Colours & Icons
Organise assignments by subject with custom colours and icons. Your dashboard tells the full story at a glance.
↩️
Complete & Undo
Mark assignments done with one tap. Accidentally completed something? Undo it anytime — no time limit, no questions asked.
⚙️
Live Seconds Countdown
Toggle a live ticking H M S countdown for assignments under 24 hours. Or extend it to all assignments for maximum urgency.
👻
Ghost Town Empty State
When your dashboard is clear, you get Ghost Town. A gentle reminder that a clean slate is yours to fill — or enjoy.
📱
Works Offline, Always
No internet required. Your data lives on your device. No account, no sync, no cloud. Just your assignments, always there.
