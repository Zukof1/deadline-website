Privacy
Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version: Deadline collects absolutely nothing. Your data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no tracking. Zero.

What data do we collect?

None. Deadline does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever.

All data you enter into Deadline — your subjects, assignments, due dates, and settings — is stored locally on your device using your phone's built-in storage. It never leaves your device.

Do we use analytics or tracking?

No. Deadline contains no analytics tools, no tracking SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party services of any kind. We have no way of knowing how many people use the app, what they do in it, or who they are.

Do we use advertising?

No. Deadline contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs. There are no ad networks integrated into the app.

Do we require an account or login?

No. Deadline requires no account, no email address, no login of any kind. You open the app and use it immediately.

Do we share data with third parties?

No. There is no data to share. We have no user data, no usage data, and no device data. Nothing is shared with anyone because nothing is collected.

What happens if I delete the app?

All your data is permanently deleted when you uninstall Deadline. Since everything is stored locally on your device, removing the app removes everything. There are no backups on our end because we have no servers.

Children's privacy

Deadline does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page and the date at the top. Given our current approach of collecting nothing, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at deadlineapp.contact@gmail.com
